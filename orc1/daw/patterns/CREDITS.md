# Pattern tiles: credits and licences

The VISUALS background can use three sets of repeating tiles. The tiles in
`hero/` and `monster/` were made by other people and are used under their
licences, below. They were converted by `extract_patterns.py` in this folder
(recoloured to white/grey on black, a black background added, Pattern
Monster outlines given a fixed width); the ORC's palette colours them on
screen. The built-in set is drawn by `orc_visuals.py` (`make_pattern`).
