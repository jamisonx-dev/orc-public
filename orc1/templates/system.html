DAW / DJ MODE—
DAW = REAPER, DJ = Mixxx, fullscreen on the monitor on the Pi's HDMI port (USB mouse + keyboard). In DJ mode the Mixtrack belongs to Mixxx; the ORC takes it back when Mixxx quits. Skipback keeps recording either way. The synths, drums and sampler stop while the app runs and restart when it quits. KEEP ENGINES leaves them running, which uses more memory. STOP asks the app to quit like its close box: unsaved edits (REAPER) or a playing deck (Mixxx) get a question on the monitor first.

HUD—
OPEN PHONE HUD ›
Live play-state on the HDMI monitor: tempo, bar / beat / step, the key and chord, every sequencer track with its playhead, which modules are sounding, held notes. Light on purpose (no browser). VISUALS, DAW and DJ share the monitor: starting one of them replaces the HUD.
