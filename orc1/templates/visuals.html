LOGO · used by the LOGO and SHAPE4D scenes
LOGO draws it turning in 3D with a bright trace along its lines; SHAPE4D turns its outline into a 4D shape (CHORD SHAPE = the held chord's shape instead). Upload SVG line art, 2 MB max: fills become outlines, text and images are ignored. Only logos you uploaded can be deleted.

SOURCE
DRAG SETS THE BAND FOR
Drag across the spectrum to pick what counts as a beat (drag an edge to adjust). The shapes and the background share one band until you pick BACKGROUND: it then gets its OWN BAND (cyan, starting as a copy of the shapes' band) and its own SENS BG. The dotted outlines are what each detector really hears: it listens to only the newest 21 ms so it can react fast, which blurs the bass into ~47 Hz steps.
LINES
TRAILS
Each frame the last picture is faded and kept: those ghosts are the trails. TRAIL ZOOM and TRAIL TWIST move only the ghosts, never the shape itself, so they show only when TRAILS is up. (To resize or turn the shape, use SIZE and ANGLE.)
MOVEMENT BEAT
LFO
LFO
REACT = how much the beat moves it: drum kicks and the audio beat, shaped by LISTEN's ATTACK and RELEASE, plus loudness (separate from LINES' BEAT: they react even with BEAT at 0). LFO = how far the LFO swings it. Both at 0 = off. SYNC locks the LFO to the ORC's bar while the sequencer runs (at its tempo when stopped); FREE uses RATE.
BACKGROUND
A repeating pattern behind the lines. It rests at black and the music brings it in; its gaps always stay black.
PATTERN
Your own tiles (SVG, 512 KB max, up to 128). Draw ONE repeat that wraps seamlessly (what leaves the right edge comes back on the left, same for top and bottom), in white or grey on black or transparent: the ORC adds the colour and reads only brightness (the red part of each colour, so pure blue shows nothing). Black stays black on screen. Same file name = replaces your earlier one.

MIDI
EVERY CONTROL'S CC NUMBER
Every slider is a MIDI CC on the visuals module's channel, so any controller (or a Fire knob set to the visuals module) can move it too. The visuals also follow the ORC's MIDI: drum kicks pulse, snares flash, the held chord sets the colour.
