CONTROLS (mapped actions)
MAPPING — connect an action to a CC

Set a CC (1–31 or 64–95) next to any action; blank = unmapped. At most 16 mapped (0/16). Saving updates the ORC's control map immediately.

To make the mapping take effect on the RC itself: put the unit in USB STORAGE mode, run bash ~/rc505_tools/rc505_write.sh, then power-cycle it.
