Protected Channels

A protected channel can't be picked anywhere in the ORC: module bindings here, pedal targets, BeatBuddy / chord / voice-thru outs, route rules, Fire knob and fader targets. Tap a channel to switch it. Things already saved on it stay as they are. 13 is the ORC's own control channel and is always protected.
