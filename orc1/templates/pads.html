Give any MIDI device's pads (or notes / CCs) their own job: keep playing what they play, play a different note on a named module, send a CC, or run an action (the same list as the FCB pedal). Pads you don't list pass through untouched. A device is matched by name. Turn on LEARN and hit a pad to add it.

Pads are triggers: an action fires on the hit, not on release, and a second hit within 80 ms is ignored as a double-trigger. Hold-style actions aren't offered. A skipback grab while held and a momentary CC need a device that sends a real release (a keyboard, most pad controllers); many drum brains send their note-off on a timer instead.
