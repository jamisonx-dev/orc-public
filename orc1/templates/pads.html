Give any MIDI device's pads (or notes / CCs) their own job: keep playing what they play, play a different note on a named module, send a CC, or run an action (the same list as the FCB pedal). Pads you don't list pass through untouched. A device is matched by name. Turn on LEARN and hit a pad to add it.

Pads are triggers: an action fires on the hit, not on release, and a second hit within 80 ms is ignored as a double-trigger. Hold-style actions aren't offered.

A surface (our Fire firmware, "ORC FW ...") gets this layout whenever it shows up, but stays on its own sequencer until you ask: press SHOW ON FIRE here, or BROWSER > ORC surface on the Fire. Then each pad (pad 0 = top left .. 63) does what you set here and shows the colour you pick; a held pad lights brighter. Pads you haven't listed are silent. The surface talks to the ORC directly and uses no MIDI channel, so the Fire's own sequencer keeps every channel and its notes always pass through untouched. Tick Surface first, then SHOW ON FIRE, LEARN and tap pads. On the Fire, any mode button (STEP / NOTE / DRUM / PERFORM) goes back to the sequencer.
