KIKPAD —

These maps are stored on the pad itself, so it plays them into any USB host (a box driving the MPC, a laptop), not only the ORC. Everything shown here is read back from the pad. An edit sounds straight away and the pad saves it a couple of seconds later.

PADS · CLICK ONE TO EDIT · LAID OUT AS ON THE PAD (ENCODERS AT THE BOTTOM)

N = note, CC = momentary CC (127 / 0), TG = toggle CC. A small number under it = its own channel; none = the bank channel.

ENCODERS · BANKS 0, 1 AND 3 · THE BOTTOM-ROW BUTTON PICKS THE MODE

Each encoder sends its CC (counting up and down from 0-127) on its channel. In bank 0, UP adds 8 to the channel.

BACK TO THE FACTORY MAPS

Bank 0's factory map is the MPC1000 face (channel 14), bank 1 the bass layout in 4ths. Bank 3 (faders) and the sequencer (bank 4) are not affected.
