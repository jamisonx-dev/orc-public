CHORD ENGINE OUTPUT
The preset sets which channel each chord voice plays on. PORT (and CH) send all external chord voices to one output instead (RTP, DIN, etc).
PORT CH
PERFORM-VE · HARDTUNE KEY
Sends chords to the TC-Helicon Perform-VE (ch 1) so its HardTune NaturalPlay re-keys the lead to what you play. It follows major/minor only (not harmonic minor or pentatonic). FOLLOW mirrors the ORC chord engine. SOURCE takes an external USB/DIN chord stream for the box and consumes it (those notes won't also play a module or pass through). Send at least a root and a 3rd — a single note won't re-key it.
SOURCE CH
CHORD VOICE MAP
Override the routing of individual voice slots on top of the active preset. preset = follow the preset; off = silence that slot. The PRESET ROUTING column shows what the active preset does for each slot.
SAVE AS
Saves the current overrides plus the active preset as this slot. The slot becomes the active preset and is kept across restarts.
MELODY SNAP OUTPUT
Melody-snap notes (played on CH15) go out the pisound on CH15 by default. Set a port and channel to send them elsewhere.
PORT CH
SYNTH BUS MIDI THRU
Copies a synth-bus channel to an external port, e.g. drums to the MPC on CH 10. Notes and CCs are forwarded. Changes apply immediately and are saved.
