ABOUT
Skipback is an always-on recorder: each track continuously records an audio source into its own ring, and you grab the last N seconds of one track, or of several at once with GRAB ALL, to ~/recordings.

RAM tracks live in memory and start empty after a reboot; they share the RAM budget (top meter). CARD tracks live on a separate ring partition (second meter) and cost no RAM. With PERSIST their history survives a reboot or power cut (the last second or so may be lost); FRESH starts them empty on each boot. Switching a track between RAM and CARD clears its audio. A CARD track will not arm while the partition is not mounted.

AUTO follows the rig (Zoom L-12 master when present, else pisound in, else the card's first stereo input); MANUAL pins a source you choose. Changes apply within about 5 s of SAVE.
