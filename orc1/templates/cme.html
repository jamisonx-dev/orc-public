CME —

Everything here is read back from the box itself. The ORC treats the CME as a patchbay: its ports are never engine inputs, so route MIDI through it here (or with its own button / UxMIDI Tools). Changes save on the box straight away.

PRESET · ROUTER, FILTERS AND MAPPERS SWITCH TOGETHER

From the FCB pedal or any pad action, cmd:CME_PRESET steps to the next preset.

ROUTER · THIS PRESET · CLICK TO CONNECT

Rows = where MIDI comes IN, columns = where it goes OUT. USB = the box's three ports on the Pi; MIDI = its DIN sockets.

FILTERS · THIS PRESET · LIT = PASSES

Pick a port, then click to block or pass. A dot marks ports with a filter. Ports marked * have not been verified on the hardware.

MAPPERS · THIS PRESET · CLICK ONE TO EDIT

A mapper changes matching MIDI on its way through the box (for example pad aftertouch to CCs). A mapper with no input is switched off.

CURVES · USED BY A MAPPER'S "CURVE" OPTION

Input 0-127 left to right, output bottom to top. The box stores 4; edit them in UxMIDI Tools.
