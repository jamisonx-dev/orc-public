FIRE UNIVERSAL PAGES are for the Akai Fire running the ORC Fire firmware (v0.31 or newer): the pages are stored ON the Fire, so they work with no Pi. FIRE KNOB MAP and CHORD ROW ROUTING apply only to a Fire running Akai's own firmware. The ORC surface pads are set on PADS.
